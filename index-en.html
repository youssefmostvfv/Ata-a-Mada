About Us

A Leading Saudi Company in Contracting & Insulation

We are Ata'a Mada for Contracting, Maintenance, Operation, and Insulation Materials. Our headquarters are in Riyadh, serving all regions of the Kingdom in the field of supplying all building materials. We began specializing in insulation materials, and now provide all construction needs, including rock wool, glass wool, PVC pipes, MDF wood, plastic, insulation boards, leak sealants, fiber mesh, MCU coatings, chemical concrete treatments, nails, plaster mesh, block ladders, Isomax and Saptex insulation angles, Hordy of all sizes, blocks, soft burlap, tying wire, bitumen, ceramic rollers, high-quality adhesive, water stop, and grout. We are proud to serve you.
